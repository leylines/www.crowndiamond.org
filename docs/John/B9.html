The Meek Shall Inherit:
Both Old and New,
God's Earth is Thiers
Matthew 5:5
 
The Celestial Realm is the seat of the Land of Promise. Some think the saying that the meek shall inherit the earth means that earth's political structures collapse, and that the thousand-year reign of Messiah will end the rule of man by man. After man has grown comfortable with the way of peace, satanic forces will be released from confinement to test the remnant. It's written that God did not make earth in vain, but made it to be inhabited, so I'll not argue directly against the prevailing view.

My pointis this. Y'shua said, "the words I speak to you are Spirit and Life." It occurs to me, therefore, that the earth of which the Spirit speaks in the beatitudes may not be planet Earth. It shall wax old, and a new heaven and a new earth are promised.

Is the new Earth an improvement over the old? Is it the old, minus the curse error brought upon it for our sakes? Eye has not seen, nor ear heard the things that are prepared. It seems to me, whatever the configuration of the promised land, the habitat will be what HaShem envisioned from beginning as Heaven and Earth were created.
